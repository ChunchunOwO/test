## Export contract

- Dimensions: `1560 x 624` pixels (`2x` the 780-pixel Steam description column).
- Format: optimized RGB PNG.
- Locale suffix: `schinese` for Steam's Simplified Chinese localization grouping.
- Recommended order: local library, lyrics, audio control, Workshop.
- Do not reuse the Simplified Chinese exports as the English, Japanese, Korean, or Traditional Chinese localization.

## Provenance

- Layout, typography, stickers, waveform decoration, and privacy-safe demo content were composed deterministically during asset preparation.
- Simplified Chinese headings and body copy use the local Noto Sans SC variable font with explicit weights for consistent, legible exports.
- The pastel room illustration was generated specifically for ECHO from an original prompt. The library capsule was used only as a palette and rounded-sticker style reference; its mascot does not appear in these banners.
- The supplied lyrics screenshot was used only as a full-screen composition reference. Its characters, artwork, song title, artist name, and lyrics were not copied.
- EQ and Workshop panels use local ECHO QA captures; those temporary source captures are not distribution assets.
- Library and lyrics panels use original synthetic demo names and original sample lyric lines; they contain no user library paths, real album artwork, artist imagery, or copied lyrics.
- No third-party logos, endorsements, review quotes, or award claims are included.

Uploading these files, inserting them into the Steam description, saving metadata, and publishing metadata are separate actions. Always inspect the Steamworks pending diff before publication.
